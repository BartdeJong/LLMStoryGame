# Story brief

Story id: paddingtonhat

Theme: Paddington Bear and his friends. Kindness, friendship and a happy surprise.

Setting: Windsor Gardens and the streets nearby in London, on a sunny summer
Saturday.

Tone: Happy and bright. Nothing scary and no sad moments. One length only:
Medium, about 30-45 minutes.

Language: Simple English for a native Dutch speaker with dyslexia. Use short
sentences and common words. Avoid idioms, slang and long or rare words.

Twist wishes: The strange things that happen are all part of a happy surprise.

Things to avoid: Violence, danger, real crimes, anyone being sad or in trouble,
long sentences and hard words.
