# Story bible: Lantern Tide (LONG version)

Status: PROPOSED, awaiting user review. Id: `lantern-tide`.
Brief: drifting village of lighthouse keepers; cozy exploration and mystery; a lost memory; no graphic violence.
Working title: **Lantern Tide** (title was left open).

## 1. Premise

Hearthdrift is a village of twelve lighthouses on linked islets that drift slowly along the Lantern Tide, a warm current that carries glowing remembrance oil. Keepers press that oil from memories that are freely given, and burn it in their lamps. At nightfall the village ties up and the lights come on, one by one.

The player arrives on the ferry as an apprentice keeper with a lost year of memory and a half-sewn name tag. Nobody in the village seems to mind that the harbour has thirteen berths and only twelve lights. Every seventh night the great Mothlight flickers, as if answering a lamp nobody can see. Oil is going missing from the press. The Hush, a fog that smothers lights, is due again soon.

## 2. The central mystery and its full solution

**Mystery:** Why is there a thirteenth berth with no light, why is oil vanishing, and what did the player forget?

**Solution:**
- Seven years ago Hearthdrift drifted into the Hush. Lamps went out and the village could not steer.
- Wren Tallow, young keeper of Berth Thirteen, lit a new lamp and fed it her own memories as oil. It was bright enough to guide the village out. Her apprentice (the player, then called Spark) fed it a year of their own memories to help.
- Burning memories also makes the giver fade from everyone else's memory. This was an unintended side effect. The village forgot Wren, and the player forgot a year.
- Wren is not dead. The Thirteenth Light sank below the tide and became the Drowned Lamp in the Lantern Deep. Wren still keeps it there, alone and unremembered. Its pull on the tide is the "missing oil". It is nearly out of oil, just as the Hush returns.
- One person remembers: Elder Hobb Gantry, Wren's grandfather. He wore her locket, which anchored his memory. He stayed silent because he feared that speaking her name would put the lamp out. It does the opposite. Remembered together, she is relit.
- Memories burnt for the lamp can return when told aloud at the lamp. The player can bring Wren home, keep the light with her, or leave it for now.

## 4. Cast (14 named, plus the player)

The player is "Spark" (the name is hidden until CT9). Species is chosen by the sprite.

1. **Hester Wick**, hedgehog, Keeper of the Mothlight.
   - Personality: warm, anxious.
   - Goal: stop the Mothlight flickering.
   - Secret: she dreams of a girl lighting a lamp.
   - Relationships: mentors the player; old friend of Hobb.
   - Truths known: CT1 (A), CT6 (C), ST6.
   - Goal resolved: when the lamp-room clue is found and she relights a steady flame, she gives the Mothlight Ember.
   - Voice: gentle, trailing "oh, dear" worries.
2. **Elder Hobb Gantry**, badger, Council Elder.
   - Personality: dignified, tired.
   - Goal: keep the village calm until the Hush passes.
   - Secret: he remembers Wren (his granddaughter) and wears her locket.
   - Relationships: grandfather of Wren; wary of Pell.
   - Truths known: CT5, CT6, CT10; suspects CT8.
   - Goal resolved: confronted with the locket or letters, he confesses and gives the Chorus rite hint.
   - Voice: formal, short sentences, long silences. Not essential: CT10 has two object routes.
3. **Pell Tipple**, magpie, collector.
   - Personality: chatty, sly, soft-hearted.
   - Goal: find the brass key that "belongs to something".
   - Secret: he collects lost things because he feels something is missing. He holds Wren's spyglass and Tallow Key.
   - Relationships: rivals with Rook; red-herring suspect.
   - Truths known: ST2.
   - Goal resolved: he gives the Tallow Key or Spyglass in trade for a shiny find.
   - Voice: fast, "finders keepers, friend".
4. **Dorrin Marsh**, heron, fog-watcher at Foghorn Rock.

   - Secret: he counted thirteen lights as a chick and was told he was wrong.

## 6. Twists, red herrings and subplots

**Twists (3):**
- **TW1, "You were her apprentice."** Setup: the half-sewn name tag, Lumi's "Spark!", the apprentice cot. Payoff: reading the Attic cot (CT9).
- **TW2, "The thief is a lamp."** Setup: Pell and Bramwell under suspicion, oil tally dropping. Payoff: the Kelp Garden amber threads show the oil flowing to the Deep (CT7).
- **TW3, "The villain remembers."** Setup: Hobb's locked study and brusque warnings. Payoff: the locket and letters (CT10). He was protecting the lamp, not hiding a crime.

**Red herrings (5):**
- RH1: Pell steals oil. In truth he collects lost things.
- RH2: A ghost light in the Whisper Fog Edge. In truth it is glow-moths.
- RH3: Rook's midnight rowing. In truth he leaves a wick at Berth Thirteen.
- RH4: The Wreck of the Pilchard has a "cursed lamp". In truth it holds old signalling mirrors.
- RH5: Hobb's locked study. In truth it holds a locket and letters.

**Subplots (5)** (not required for any core truth):
- SP1, Thirteen Buns: Granny's missing spice. Reward: Warm Bun (a memory trigger for the Flask). Payoff: ST1.
- SP2, Banner of Stars: Yarrow's torn banner. Reward: Tallow Key.
- SP3, Pell's Hoard: return lost things to owners. Reward: Brass Spyglass.
- SP4, Mothlight Flicker: Hester and Dorrin. Reward: Mothlight Ember and ST6.
- SP5, Ledger Repair: Tamsin and Dusk Ink. Reward: a complete ledger for the epilogue.

## 7. Endings and what decides them

The deciding choice is made at Wren's Lamp Room. It is unlocked by CT8 and CT11.

- **E1, The Thirteenth Dawn** (best).
  - Conditions: all 11 core truths, and the choice "Bring her home".
  - Scene: the village gathers at the Berth and tells Wren's name and a memory each. The lamp is relit above the tide, Wren returns, and the player regains their year.
  - Tone: joyful. Epilogue notes vary with resolved goals.
- **E2, The Shared Light.**
  - Conditions: all 11 core truths, and the choice "Keep the light together".
  - Scene: Wren stays as the lamp's keeper. The village remembers her and tends the Drowned Lamp as a thirteenth light on the tide.
  - Tone: bittersweet and warm.
- **E3, Drift On.**
  - Conditions: CT8 found and the choice "Not yet", or leaving before CT11.
  - Scene: the lamp holds one more year and the Hush passes. The village stays cozy but forgetful, and the player keeps one lantern burning for her.
  - Tone: gentle and wistful. A fallback ending.
- **E4, Thirteen Lanterns** (secret).
  - Conditions: E1 conditions plus SP1 to SP5 resolved.
  - Scene: every villager brings a lantern, the banner flies with thirteen stars, and the Hush never comes back.
  - Tone: triumphant.

## 8. Style guide

- **Narrator voice:** warm, second person, present tense, short sentences, with soft humour and lamp and tide imagery.
- **Naming:** plain first names (Hester, Pell, Nim) with surnames from lamp and sea trades (Wick, Tallow, Quay, Reed). Places read like homes or chores (Kettle Quay, Thirteen Buns).
- **Use:** lantern, wick, oil, drift, tide, hush, mooring, glow, hum, cosy, keeper.
- **Avoid:** blood, kill, dead, corpse, curse, gore, weapon, modern technology, clinical words ("amnesia"). Say "lost" or "faded". Never use the word "forgotten" as a threat.
