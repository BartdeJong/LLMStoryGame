# Story bible prompt

Use the supplied brief to write a proposed story bible for the Long version.
Save it as `stories/bibles/<story-id>.md`. Do not generate pack JSON yet.

Cover:
- Premise and central mystery, including its complete, satisfying solution.
- Ordered core truths and their dependencies.
- Cast: name, species, personality, goal, secret, motivations, relationships,
  truths known, goal resolution, and voice notes.
- Locations, their story roles, and how the player can travel between them.
- Twists and red herrings, including setup and payoff.
- Ending outlines and the conditions needed to reach them.

Keep the story cozy, in English, and free of graphic violence. Every core truth
must ultimately have two independent routes for discovery. Do not make a
character essential when their goal could be blocked. Mark unresolved choices
as questions for the user rather than silently deciding them.
