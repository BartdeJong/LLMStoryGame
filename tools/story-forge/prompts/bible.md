# Story bible prompt

Use the supplied brief to write a proposed story bible for the Long version.
Save it as `stories/bibles/<story-id>.md`. Do not generate pack JSON yet.

Cover:
- Premise and central mystery, including its complete, satisfying solution.
- Ordered core truths and their dependencies.
- Cast: name, species, personality, goal, secret, motivations, relationships,
  truths known, goal resolution, and voice notes.
- Locations, their story roles, and how the player can travel between them.
- Twists and red herrings, including setup and payoff.
- For key truths, the deduction question the player answers, the right answer,
  2-3 plausible wrong answers, which red herrings the answer rules out, and the
  items and clues that prove the right answer, with how many of them must be
  shown (use two only when the answer rests on two sources).
- For each ending, the 2-4 summation questions and the truth each one needs,
  and the evidence that proves one central question per ending.
- Statements (optional): for some characters, something they say that is false
  or incomplete, why they hide it (from their secret or motivations), the
  evidence that breaks it, what they admit and which clue that reveals. Aim for
  at least 6, spread over different characters, with at least one on a route to
  a core truth.
- For each ending, a flawed variant: the same facts, but something is left
  unsettled or someone else has to put it right.
- Ending outlines and the conditions needed to reach them.

Keep the story cozy, in English, and free of graphic violence. Every core truth
must ultimately have two independent routes for discovery. Do not make a
character essential when their goal could be blocked. Mark unresolved choices
as questions for the user rather than silently deciding them.
